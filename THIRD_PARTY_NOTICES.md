## Astronomy

The sun and moon positions follow the published low-precision formulas in Jean Meeus, *Astronomical Algorithms* (2nd ed., 1998), chapters 25 and 47. Sunrise and sunset follow the NOAA solar calculator method. No code was copied from either.
